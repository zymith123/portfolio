How I approach a workflow

  1. Discover

    Map the repetitive process and where time is lost.

  2. Analyze

    Identify which steps are rule-based and safe to automate.

  3. Design

    Plan a workflow around your existing tools and data.

  4. Automate

    Build it in n8n with APIs, webhooks and AI where useful.

  5. Integrate

    Connect it to the apps, sheets and inboxes you already use.

  6. Deploy

    Put it live with error handling and notifications.

  7. Improve

    Monitor real runs and refine the logic over time.

Prototypes

Workflows I’ve built

Personal prototypes built on an n8n trial while developing this skill set. They are not running in production, but they show the kind of workflow I put together.

Prototype

AI Job Discovery

Runs daily. An Apify actor scrapes OnlineJobs.ph listings; an AI step scores each role for fit and explains its reasoning; a second AI step drafts a tailored cover letter; results land in a Google Sheet.

Full case study →

Prototype

Lead Generation

A form kicks off an Apify actor that pulls business leads from Google Maps. The workflow de-duplicates and standardizes them into a Google Sheet, scrapes each site for emails and socials, uses AI to extract and format the details, and an AI agent drafts an outreach email per lead.

Prototype

Upwork Proposal Assistant

Pulls a job posting and my CV, then an AI agent drafts a tailored proposal and cover letter aligned to that specific job. The draft is saved as a document and a Gmail draft to review and send — it does not apply on its own.

Prototype

Auto-sort Google Drive Files

When a new file lands in Drive, the workflow downloads it, an AI step analyzes the document and decides which folder it belongs in, then it finds that folder and moves the file.
