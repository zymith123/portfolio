Lead Generation Automation Prototype

An n8n workflow that turns a form submission into a list of qualified local business leads — scraped from Google Maps, enriched with contact details, and paired with an AI-drafted outreach email for each one.

Personal project 2026 n8n · Apify · Google Gemini

The problem

Finding and reaching out to local business leads by hand means the same research loop every time: search for businesses, visit each website to find a contact, and write a personalized opening message. I wanted a workflow that handles the finding, the enriching, and the first-draft outreach automatically, leaving me to just review and send.

What I built

  • Find New Leads — a form submission triggers an Apify actor that pulls business leads from Google Maps, checks them against previously saved leads, filters out duplicates and standardizes the output, then appends the new ones to a Google Sheet.
  • Find Emails and Socials — filters leads down to the ones with a website, scrapes each site, keeps the ones that scraped successfully, runs an AI step that extracts contact details and formats them to JSON, then updates each lead's row in the sheet.
  • Generate Outreach Email — filters for rows that now have an email, passes each one to an AI agent backed by a Google Gemini chat model to draft a personalized outreach email, and writes the draft back to the sheet.

Technology

n8nApify Google SheetsGoogle Gemini

Key technical decisions

  • Three scoped stages, not one long chain. Splitting "find leads," "enrich contact details," and "generate outreach" into separate groups keeps each stage independently testable and re-runnable without repeating the earlier ones.
  • The sheet is the shared state. Every stage reads from and writes back to the same Google Sheet by row, so a lead's progress — found, enriched, emailed — is always visible in one place rather than held only in the workflow's internal memory.
  • A filter gates every stage. Before scraping, before AI enrichment, and before drafting an email, a filter checks the row actually has what that stage needs (a website, a successfully scraped page, an email), so the AI steps only run on leads that are ready for them.
  • An AI agent drafts the email, not a single prompt. The outreach step uses an AI agent — with a Gemini chat model, memory, and tools — rather than a one-shot prompt, so each email is actually written per lead instead of templated.

Status

Built and tested in n8n (see the demo below). It runs on a personal n8n trial rather than a paid or production instance, so it's not processing live traffic continuously.
