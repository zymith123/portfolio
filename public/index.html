I’m Jerwin Babatugon, a software developer based in the Philippines. I build and maintain web applications that businesses rely on day to day, and I design automations that take repetitive manual work off people’s plates. Automation & AI

Automating repetitive business processes

I build workflows that connect the tools a business already uses, so information moves on its own instead of by hand. This is a growing part of my work, built on my software development background.

What I connect

Business applications APIs AI models Databases Google Sheets Webhooks n8n Apify Email Notifications

How I approach a workflow

  1. Discover

    Map the repetitive process and where time is lost.

  2. Analyze

    Identify which steps are rule-based and safe to automate.

  3. Design

    Plan a workflow around your existing tools and data.

  4. Automate

    Build it in n8n with APIs, webhooks and AI where useful.

  5. Integrate

    Connect it to the apps, sheets and inboxes you already use.

  6. Deploy

    Put it live with error handling and notifications.

  7. Improve

    Monitor real runs and refine the logic over time.
